June – July 2026

Consulting Micro-Placement · Augimono

I completed a client consultancy placement focused on adapting Augimono’s existing gamified behavioural-learning model into a workforce-readiness proposition for apprentices and recent graduates.

My Contribution

I carried out research, helped structure the recommendation framework, developed worked operational scenarios, and took primary responsibility for producing the final client report. The work translated an open-ended brief into practical product, measurement, accessibility, and commercial recommendations.

Key Outputs

  • Five design principles for adapting the existing behavioural loop to workforce-readiness use cases.
  • A three-level measurement framework for decision-making, confidence, and operational readiness.
  • Three worked scenarios designed as practical build specifications for a future product release.
  • Recommendations covering neurodivergent accessibility and enterprise technology considerations.
  • Careful commercial framing around approved provision and the Growth and Skills Levy.
Live impact: The client confirmed that the design principles have entered its product language, the measurement framework is being used as the blueprint for an Operational Readiness Report, and the three worked scenarios are progressing into development for the next release.

What I Learned

The placement strengthened my ability to work from an ambiguous client brief, separate evidence from assumption, prioritise commercially useful recommendations, and communicate research in a form that can directly guide product development.
