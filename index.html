About

I’m entering the final year of a BEng in Electronic and Computer Engineering at the University of Nottingham. I’m interested in engineering projects that sit between hardware and software: embedded systems, electronics, robotics, FPGA-based design, signal processing, and software tools that interact with real-world systems.

My projects include a C++/Qt CAD visualisation system, an embedded line-following robot with PID control and Node-RED monitoring, a pulse oximeter prototype involving signal acquisition and FPGA subsystem work, and a client consultancy project whose recommendations are now informing a live product release.

I’m currently looking for graduate, internship, and early-career opportunities where I can apply practical engineering, programming, debugging, and hardware-software integration skills to real products and systems.
